HTRU2
======
Author: Robert Lyon, University of Manchester.
Dataset citation: Lyon, R. (2015). HTRU2 [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning
Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5DK6R
Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/372/htru2
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

HTRU2 is redistributed here as selected rows and four selected feature columns
with fixed teaching splits. Numerical feature values and class labels are
unchanged. The source row indices are included in htru2_small.npz.

Please also cite the source paper:
R. J. Lyon, B. W. Stappers, S. Cooper, J. M. Brooke, and J. D. Knowles,
"Fifty Years of Pulsar Candidate Selection: From simple filters to a new
principled real-time classification approach", MNRAS, 2016.

Source acknowledgement: the observational data were collected by the High Time
Resolution Universe Collaboration using the Parkes Observatory, funded by the
Commonwealth of Australia and managed by the CSIRO. HTRU2 acknowledges support
from EPSRC grant EP/I028099/1 at the University of Manchester.

Fashion-MNIST
=============
Authors: Han Xiao, Kashif Rasul, and Roland Vollgraf, Zalando Research.
Paper: Fashion-MNIST: a Novel Image Dataset for Benchmarking Machine Learning
Algorithms (2017). https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.07747
Source: https://github.com/zalandoresearch/fashion-mnist
License: MIT. https://github.com/zalandoresearch/fashion-mnist/blob/master/LICENSE

The course subset contains original 28 x 28 uint8 images from three classes.
Image values are unchanged. Original class labels 0, 1, and 8 are remapped to
0, 1, and 2. Training/validation images come from the official training set;
test images come from the official test set. Original row indices are included.
